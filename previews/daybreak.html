The thesis

The category assumes you show up.

That assumption fails exactly when the product matters most. The week someone stops sleeping is the week they stop opening a meditation app. A library cannot help a person who has gone quiet.

So Prelua does not wait to be opened. It reads the signals you generate anyway — what you engage with, when your phone goes quiet, how your check-ins trend — and reaches you first.

The mechanism

A hidden variable, reverse-calculated.

It never asks how well you are. It derives a Wellness Score — nought to a hundred, recalculated daily — from five weighted signal families, and lets that, not a stated preference, decide what arrives next.

Scientific foundation

Not invented. Implemented.

Prelua is a direct implementation of JITAI — Just-In-Time Adaptive Interventions — the intervention-design framework from behavioural health research. Deliver the right support at the right moment by adapting to a person’s changing state, rather than on a fixed schedule.

The framework has been evaluated across smoking cessation, physical activity, alcohol use, stress management and mental-health support, and underpins NIH-funded trials. We did not invent a mechanism and go looking for evidence.

Where we draw the line

A system that watches you
is held to a higher standard.

Prelua does not diagnose, does not claim clinical outcomes, and is not a crisis service. Crisis language is escalated to resources, never coached. Users aged 13 to 17 are permanently excluded from cross-user pattern learning. No advertising, no data sale — the business model is a subscription and nothing else.
