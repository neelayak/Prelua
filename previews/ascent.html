Every wellness app on your phone is a content library that waits. Prelua builds a living model of your mental wellness from the signals you already generate — then acts on it.

The thesis

The category assumes you show up.

That fails exactly when the product matters most. The week someone stops sleeping is the week they stop opening a meditation app. A library cannot help a person who has gone quiet.

So Prelua does not wait to be opened. It reaches you first.

The mechanism

A hidden variable, reverse-calculated.

It never asks how well you are. It derives a Wellness Score — nought to a hundred, recalculated daily — and lets that, not a stated preference, decide what arrives next.

Three days of Tired · Work stress, four of five meditations completed, bedtimes drifting later: 59.5 out of 100. Vulnerable. Delivery shifts toward stress relief and sleep support before the next check-in says anything is wrong.

Scientific foundation

Not invented. Implemented.

A direct implementation of JITAI — Just-In-Time Adaptive Interventions — the intervention-design framework from behavioural health research, evaluated across smoking cessation, physical activity, alcohol use, stress and mental-health support.
